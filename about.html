Message From The Founder

Founder & Managing Director

Sruthi Goud

Founder, Owner & Chief Executive Officer

"True luxury is never loud. It is found in the quiet perfection of intuitive service, the warmth of a genuine welcome, and spaces crafted to elevate the human spirit. At Sruthi Hotel, we treat each guest not merely as a visitor, but as an honored member of our extended family."

With over 20 years of experience shaping high-end international hospitality across Europe, Asia, and the Americas, Sruthi Goud envisioned an urban retreat where timeless architectural majesty effortlessly harmonizes with contemporary tech and wellness sanctuaries.

Under her visionary leadership, Sruthi Hotel has consistently ranked among the world's premier boutique luxury destinations, celebrated for discreet VIP privacy, sustainable luxury initiatives, and Michelin-caliber culinary excellence.

Guiding Principles

Our Mission & Vision

The enduring philosophy that directs every touchpoint of the Sruthi Hotel guest experience.

Our Mission

To provide an unparalleled sanctuary of luxury, refined tranquility, and personalized service. We strive to anticipate every guest desire before it is voiced, cultivating enduring memories through bespoke hospitality, culinary mastery, and exquisite attention to comfort.

  • Deliver flawless personalized service 24 hours a day.
  • Maintain unmatched standards of cleanliness and hygiene.
  • Create lasting bonds with our global community of guests.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the gold standard in boutique luxury hospitality. We aspire to blend heritage charm with sustainable modern innovation, continuously raising the benchmark for guest wellness, architectural grace, and social responsibility.

  • Be the most revered luxury retreat in the region.
  • Champion carbon-neutral eco-friendly hospitality practices.
  • Nurture world-class hospitality talent and leaders.
